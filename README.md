## 📂 Featured Projects

### ☕ 1. Maven Roasters — Sales & Peak Hours Analysis (Mini Project)
> **Business Impact:** Uncovered critical peak-traffic hours to optimize staffing shifts, identified cross-selling opportunities to boost Average Order Value (AOV), and streamlined multi-store sales reporting.

* **Folder Directory:** `Mini project G6/`
* **Core Tech Stack:** `SQL Server` | `Power Query` | `Power BI` | `DAX`
* **Interactive Dashboard Preview:**

![Dashboard Preview]https://github.com/omnyasamir96-star/Team_6_ITC-DAT1_G1-/blob/main/Mini%20project%20G6/GP.3.%20BI%20%26%20Dashboard/Coffee%20shop%20sales%20Dashboard.png

#### 🔑 Key Project Highlights
- **Engineered Advanced DAX Metrics:** Developed custom measures including `Total Revenue`, `AOV`, and a dynamic `Peak Hour` algorithm formatted (`HH:00`).
- **Data Pipeline & ETL:** Cleansed, transformed, and explicitly structured data types across transactional, financial, and temporal fields using **Power Query** and **SQL Server**.
- **Actionable Business Recommendations:** Identified `10:00 AM` as the primary daily revenue peak, recommending targeted staff allocation during the Morning Rush (9:00 AM – 11:00 AM) to maximize throughput and eliminate customer wait times.
